ONGOING MSc THESIS

Gendered Effects of Mobile Money on Multidimensional Poverty in Kenya

This study investigates how mobile money access and usage affect multidimensional poverty in Kenya, while examining the moderating influence of education and women's empowerment. The research applies econometric techniques using nationally representative FinAccess survey data to generate evidence that can support inclusive financial policies and sustainable development.

Published Policy Paper

Balancing Enforcement and Compliance: Rethinking Tax Governance in Kenya

This policy paper examines the relationship between tax enforcement and voluntary taxpayer compliance in Kenya. It proposes practical institutional and policy reforms that strengthen revenue collection while promoting trust, transparency and long-term compliance.
